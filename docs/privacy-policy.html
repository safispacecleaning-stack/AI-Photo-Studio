AI Photo Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-09

AI Photo Studio processes selected photos on the device. The app does not upload photos, create accounts, use advertising identifiers, or collect analytics.

Enhanced images are written to the app's temporary cache so they can be previewed inside the app. Cached files can be removed by clearing the app cache or uninstalling the app. The original photo is not overwritten.

The app uses Android system photo selection and storage APIs. Android may show its own permission and picker disclosures. No photo is shared with the developer or a third party by the app.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the publisher at micahomwenga06@gmail.com.
